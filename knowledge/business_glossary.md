# NovaMart Business Glossary

## Revenue

Revenue is the total monetary value generated from completed customer orders.

Revenue should normally be analyzed by:

- Month
- Product
- Region
- Product category

Cancelled and pending orders should not be treated as completed revenue.

---

## Order

An order represents a customer purchase transaction.

An order can contain one or more products.

Order status may include:

- Completed
- Pending
- Cancelled

---

## Return

A return occurs when a customer sends a purchased product back.

Return count measures the number of recorded returns.

Return rate is calculated as:

Return Rate = Total Returns / Completed Orders × 100

Return rate is more useful than raw return count when comparing periods with different order volumes.

---

## Support Ticket

A support ticket represents a customer interaction with NovaMart support.

Support tickets can belong to categories such as:

- Complaint
- Refund
- Payment
- Delivery
- Product Question
- Technical Issue

---

## Complaint

A complaint is a support interaction where the customer reports dissatisfaction with a product, service, delivery, or experience.

---

## Refund

A refund-related ticket concerns money being returned to the customer.

---

## Technical Issue

A technical issue is a customer-reported problem involving the technical operation or functionality of a product or service.

---

## Revenue Anomaly

A revenue anomaly is a period where revenue behaves unusually compared with historical patterns.

An anomaly is an investigation signal, not proof of a business problem.

---

## Evidence

Evidence is an observed data point or calculated metric that supports an investigation.

Examples:

- Revenue increased by 32%.
- Return rate increased by 0.67 percentage points.
- Support tickets decreased by 7.59%.

Evidence does not automatically establish causation.

---

## Causal Claim

A causal claim states that one event directly caused another event.

Example:

"Product X caused the revenue increase."

The analytics system must not make causal claims unless appropriate supporting evidence exists.
