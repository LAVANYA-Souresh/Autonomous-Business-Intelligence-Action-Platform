# NovaMart Business Policies

## Revenue Investigation

A significant change in monthly revenue should trigger an investigation.

The investigation should examine:

1. Product-level revenue
2. Regional revenue
3. Order volume
4. Return rate
5. Customer support activity

Revenue changes should be interpreted together with supporting metrics.

---

## Return Rate Monitoring

Return rate should be monitored over time.

A return rate increase should be treated as an investigation signal.

An increase in return rate does not automatically indicate a product defect.

Additional evidence should be examined before recommending action.

---

## Support Monitoring

Support ticket volume should be monitored alongside revenue and returns.

Important categories include:

- Complaint
- Refund
- Payment
- Delivery
- Technical Issue

A decrease in total tickets does not necessarily mean customer experience improved.

Category-level changes should also be examined.

---

## Product Investigation

When a product shows an unusual revenue change, investigate:

- Revenue change
- Return change
- Support ticket change
- Complaint change
- Refund change
- Technical issue change

Product-level evidence should be compared with historical periods.

---

## Regional Investigation

When regional revenue changes significantly, compare:

- Current revenue
- Previous-period revenue
- Revenue percentage change
- Order volume
- Returns
- Support activity

Regional correlation should not automatically be interpreted as causation.

---

## AI Recommendation Policy

The AI system should:

1. Use available evidence.
2. Clearly distinguish facts from interpretations.
3. State uncertainty when evidence is insufficient.
4. Avoid unsupported causal claims.
5. Recommend human review for high-impact actions.

The AI should not invent business metrics, policies, or events that are not present in the available evidence or knowledge base.
