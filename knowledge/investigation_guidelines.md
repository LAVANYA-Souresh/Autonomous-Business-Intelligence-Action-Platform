# NovaMart Investigation Guidelines

## Investigation Process

When a business anomaly is detected:

### Step 1 — Confirm the anomaly

Identify:

- Anomalous period
- Metric
- Observed value
- Historical comparison

### Step 2 — Investigate products

Compare product performance between the anomalous period and the previous period.

Look for:

- Large revenue changes
- Return increases
- Support increases
- Complaint increases
- Refund increases

### Step 3 — Investigate regions

Compare regional performance between the anomalous period and the previous period.

Identify regions with unusually large changes.

### Step 4 — Investigate returns

Calculate:

- Total returns
- Completed orders
- Return rate
- Change in return rate

Return rate should be preferred over raw return counts when comparing periods with different order volumes.

### Step 5 — Investigate customer support

Compare:

- Total support tickets
- Complaint tickets
- Refund tickets
- Payment tickets
- Delivery tickets
- Technical issue tickets

### Step 6 — Form an evidence-based assessment

The assessment should contain:

- Observed facts
- Supporting evidence
- Possible explanations
- Uncertainty
- Recommended next investigation

Do not present a possible explanation as a confirmed cause.

---

## Confidence

Use the following general interpretation:

### High confidence

Multiple independent metrics support the same observation.

### Medium confidence

Several metrics support an observation, but important evidence is missing.

### Low confidence

Only one metric supports the observation or the available evidence is weak.

Confidence refers to the strength of available evidence, not certainty about causation.

---

## Human Review

Human review should be recommended when:

- Evidence is contradictory.
- The proposed action could significantly affect customers.
- The proposed action could affect pricing.
- The proposed action could affect product availability.
- The system does not have enough evidence.
